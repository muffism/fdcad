# Round 9t group D1: draft options (D521, D522)

Design note written before the code (common brief rule 6), then updated after the build to match what was built.

Note for the merge: `tests/docs/test_user_guide.py::test_every_command_is_in_the_guide` fails on this branch until the user guide text in section c (with the words "Draft Analysis") is added to `docs/user_guide/evaluate.md`, which this group may not edit.

## a. DECISIONS entries

D521: Parting line draft and step draft (PHASE2_SPEC P2.7 item 4). (1) Draft gets an optional `draft_type`: `"neutral_plane"` (default, not written), `"parting_line"` or `"step"`. Parting line and step drafts use `refs.neutral` as the direction of pull (a plane or a flat face, Reverse flips it, as for the neutral plane method) and a new `refs.parting_lines` (edge references). `refs.faces` and `propagation` are not used by them and are not written. (2) Faces to draft are found from the edges: every face next to a parting edge is on the pull side of the edge (its centre lies further along the pull) or on the other side. Faces lying in the parting plane (square to the pull) are skipped. By default the faces on the pull side are drafted; `other_side` (optional boolean) drafts the faces on the other side instead; `both_sides` (optional boolean) drafts both, the other side by `angle2` (optional angle, the main angle when it is not written). Each side tilts away from the parting line: pull side faces lean in going along the pull, other side faces lean in going against it, so the parting line stays the widest section and stays where it is. (3) Every parting edge must lie in a plane square to the pull (straight or curved edges, checked at several points to 0.001 mm); an edge that does not is an error naming it. Parting line draft needs all edges in one such plane; edges at several heights fail with a message that points to Step Draft. (4) Method (`core/features/draft_parting.py`): the kernel's draft always carries a tilt on to faces joined smoothly, so a face split by a split line would drag its other half with it. The part is therefore cut by the parting plane into the piece on each side (a common with a box far larger than the part), the faces of each piece are drafted about the parting plane, the pieces are joined again and the faces the cut divided are merged back: only across edges in the parting plane whose two faces carry the name of one face; every other edge and vertex is kept, so the part's own split faces never merge. When the parting plane is the top or bottom of the part, the empty piece is skipped. Inside a piece the kernel still carries the tilt on to faces joined smoothly on the same side (the rest of a side face above a lower split line). When a drafted piece comes back slightly out of tolerance (split faces next to a tilted face), the kernel's shape fixer mends it, keeping the face names. Drafted faces keep their names; the feature adds no face roles. (5) Step draft: the parting edges may lie at several heights, each in a plane square to the pull (a stepped parting line). Faces are grouped by the height of their parting edges and each group is drafted about its own plane in turn, from the lowest along the pull; a face next to edges at two heights is an error naming it. The connecting faces between the levels keep their position, so the drafted faces at each level meet them in a step. (6) Errors: no direction of pull, no parting lines, a lost edge (named), a face the kernel cannot tilt (named), and a cut or join that fails ("Use a smaller angle or fewer parting lines"). (7) Panel: Type of Draft at the top (Neutral Plane, Parting Line, Step Draft; remembered for the next draft like the angle). For the two new types the Neutral Plane group is titled Direction of Pull, its button Pick Direction Face, and the Faces to Draft group is replaced by Parting Lines: a list of edges picked in the view, Other side (hidden while Draft both sides is ticked), Draft both sides and Other angle (shown only with Draft both sides, starting at the draft angle). The first flat face clicked while Pick Direction Face is on sets the direction; edges clicked go to the list; other faces are refused with "Pick edges for the parting lines, or a flat face for the direction." Show changed faces moved to the Draft Angle group so it is there for every type. (8) Where parting edges come from: FDCAD merges faces that lie on one surface when features join, so split side faces come from imported parts (STEP); in parts modelled in FDCAD the parting edges are usually where a boss meets a face (a round boss gives a curved parting line and its side becomes a cone) or an outer edge of the part. (9) Left out: parting edges on a slope (not in a plane square to the pull). The kernel's draft tilts a face about one straight line in its neutral plane and makes no new faces, so along a sloped edge the drafted face and the face across the edge no longer meet on the edge; a step face would have to be built by hand for every case, which is not reliable.
Status: Default
Reason: Cutting the part at the parting plane keeps the kernel's own reliable neutral plane draft while stopping it from carrying the tilt across the split line.

D522: Draft analysis and the draft assistant (PHASE2_SPEC P2.7 item 4). (1) Evaluate, Draft Analysis is an on and off overlay like Overhang Analysis (D354): a kernel job after every rebuild, display only, nothing saved. While it is on a small tool window shows Direction of Pull (the planes of the part, Top Plane first by default, or a flat face picked with Use Selected Face), Reverse, Minimum Angle (degrees, default 1) and a legend with the colour and face count of each group. (2) Maths (`fdcad/core/draft_analysis.py`, no Qt): the part is triangulated as for the overhang analysis and every triangle is tested in one numpy pass. A triangle's draft is the angle between its surface and the pull direction, positive when its outward normal leans toward the pull: sin(draft) = n . d. Area shares per face are summed with `numpy.bincount`. (3) A face is Straddle (blue) when at least 2 percent of its area has positive draft and at least 2 percent negative draft of at least the minimum angle; otherwise it takes the group with the largest share of its area: Positive draft (green, at least the minimum angle), Negative draft (red, at least the minimum angle) or Needs draft (yellow, draft smaller than the minimum angle either way). Faces square to the pull count as positive or negative (draft 90 degrees). (4) Draft assistant: the Faces to Draft group (neutral plane method) has Add Faces That Need Draft. It runs the same maths in a kernel job (`assistant_faces` in `core/draft_analysis.py`) on the part before the draft, with the neutral plane's normal as the pull and the draft angle as the minimum, and adds every flat, cylindrical or conical face whose group is Needs draft and that is not already in the list (the kernel can only tilt those kinds). A face square to the pull, such as a flat neutral face, is never in Needs draft. A line under the button says how many faces were added ("Added 3 faces that need draft.", "No other face needs draft from this neutral plane.") or what is missing. It only fills the list: Cancel drops it like any other pick. (5) The command sits in the Evaluate tab's Check group under Geometry Check, with its own icon and no shortcut. The tool window is a Qt tool window like the curvature graph's; the window and the colours go away when the command is turned off.
Status: Default
Reason: One set of maths serves the analysis and the assistant, and the triangle pass is the same fast numpy method the other analyses use.

## b. CHANGELOG "Added" bullets

- Draft: Parting Line and Step Draft types. Faces next to picked parting edges are drafted away from the parting line on one side, the other side or both, with the parting line kept in place (D521).
- Evaluate, Draft Analysis colours faces by their draft from a direction of pull: positive, negative, needing draft and straddle. The Draft panel can add every face that needs draft in one click (D522).

## c. User guide text

Page `docs/user_guide/modify.md`, Draft section, add:

Type of Draft chooses the method.

- Neutral Plane: faces tilt about a plane you pick.
- Parting Line: pick the direction of pull and the parting edges. The faces next to the edges on the pull side tilt about the edges, so the edges stay where they are. Tick Other side to draft the faces on the other side, or Draft both sides to draft both (Other angle sets the angle of the other side).
- Step Draft: as Parting Line, but the parting edges may lie at different heights. Each height is drafted about its own plane.

Parting edges must lie square to the direction of pull. A split part (two features stacked on the same outline) gives such edges.

Add Faces That Need Draft adds every face whose draft is smaller than the draft angle.

Page `docs/user_guide/evaluate.md`, add a Draft Analysis section:

Draft Analysis colours faces by how they release along a direction of pull. Green faces have at least the minimum angle of positive draft. Red faces have at least the minimum angle of negative draft (an undercut). Yellow faces need draft. Blue faces straddle: part of the face is positive and part negative. Pick the direction in the small window (a plane, or a flat face with Use Selected Face), and set the minimum angle. The colours are not saved.

## d. Left out

- Parting edges on a slope (D521 item 8): the kernel's draft cannot make the step faces reliably.
