# Round 9t group A: sketch shapes (D511 to D514)

Status: built. Code in `src/fdcad/ui/sketch_tools/three_point_tools.py`, `dynamic_mirror.py`, `typed_values.py` and `src/fdcad/core/sketch/curvature.py`.

## a. DECISIONS entries

D511: 3 point rectangles and parallelogram (TOOL_SPEC T2.3 item 4). (1) Three new sketch tools, each a command in the Sketch tab rectangle dropdown with Corner Rectangle and Center Rectangle: 3 Point Corner Rectangle (`sketch.three_point_corner_rectangle`), 3 Point Center Rectangle (`sketch.three_point_center_rectangle`) and Parallelogram (`sketch.parallelogram`). No default keys. (2) 3 Point Corner Rectangle: click the first corner, click the second corner (this sets the first side's length and angle, at any angle), then the third click sets the other side: the third corner is the cursor moved square onto the line through the second corner, so only its distance from the first side counts. The four lines get Perpendicular between the first two sides and Parallel on each pair of opposite sides, never Horizontal or Vertical, so the rectangle can turn. (3) 3 Point Center Rectangle: click the centre, click the midpoint of one side (this sets half the width and the angle), then the third click sets half the height (its distance from the line through the centre and that midpoint). Like Center Rectangle it gets two construction diagonals that meet in a construction centre point (Midpoint of both), plus Perpendicular between two adjacent sides; the diagonals already make opposite sides parallel. When the second click lands on an existing point, that point gets Midpoint to the side through it. (4) Parallelogram: click corner 1, corner 2, then the third click is corner 3 anywhere (the adjacent side at any angle); corner 4 completes it. Only Parallel on opposite sides. (5) Shift while placing the third point makes the two 3 point rectangles a square (both sides the length of the first) and adds Equal between two adjacent sides, as D159. (6) Corners snap and take the snap's relations as the other rectangles do; a third corner moved off its snap by the square rule is placed free. Click drag (D155) works: a press and drag gives the second point, a click the third. (7) Typed sizes (D443): after the first click a typed number sets the width (the first side's full length; for the centre tool the box asks for the full width and the side midpoint goes half of it from the centre); after the second click a typed number sets the height (the second side's length: square distance for the rectangles, along the cursor direction for the parallelogram). Each locked size adds a Distance dimension to the first or second side in the same undo step. (8) The shape values group (D157) shows Width and Height of the first two sides for all three tools, typed through the solver. (9) A shape whose three points are in a line, or with a zero side, is not made. (10) Icons are drawn in the house style. Nothing is stored in a new file key: the shapes are plain lines and relations.
Status: Default
Reason: These are the usual three point inputs; Perpendicular and Parallel keep a turned rectangle a rectangle without tying it to the sketch axes.

D512: Perimeter circle (TOOL_SPEC T2.4 item 2). (1) A new tool Perimeter Circle (`sketch.perimeter_circle`) in the Circle dropdown with Circle. Click three points on the circle. After one click a line follows the cursor; after two the circle through both clicks and the cursor follows it. (2) The third click makes a normal circle (centre point and radius) through the three points. Three points in a line give no circle: the clicks are dropped and the status bar says "The three points are in a line, so no circle goes through them. Click a point off the line." (3) Snaps: a click on an existing point (end point, centre, origin) puts that point on the circle (Coincident between the point and the circle). A click that snapped to anything else with relations (a point on a curve, a midpoint, an intersection, a part edge or vertex) places a construction point there with the snap's relations and puts it on the circle. A free click adds nothing. With automatic relations off no snap relation is added. (4) No typed size: the circle has no single size to type while its three points are picked.
Status: Default
Reason: A circle stored as centre and radius works with every existing tool, dimension and file; the snap relations keep the circle through what it was drawn through.

D513: On screen value input while drawing (TOOL_SPEC T11 item 6). (1) A new switch on Preferences > Sketch, "On screen value input while drawing", off by default, stored as `sketch/value_input` with the other sketch switches (QSettings, not in `.fdef`). (2) When on, as soon as a tool that takes typed sizes (D443: Line, Centerline, Corner and Center Rectangle, Circle, Centerpoint Arc, straight slots; and the D511 tools) is ready for a size, the D443 value box opens at the cursor by itself, empty, without a digit typed first. A line chain opens it again for each segment; a rectangle asks for the height after the width. (3) The box never blocks drawing: while it is empty it follows the cursor, and a click in the sketch closes it without locking a size and places the point as usual. Enter in the empty box just closes it. Once the user types into it, it works exactly as D443: Enter locks, a click applies the typed value as before, Escape closes it. The box has keyboard focus while open, so letter shortcuts wait until it is closed. Tools with value labels (Polygon, patterns and so on) keep their labels and do not open the box by itself. (4) Everything else is the D443 machinery (typed_size and the value box); nothing new is saved in files.
Status: Default
Reason: Reusing the D443 box keeps one way to type sizes; an empty box that gives way to clicks keeps mouse drawing unchanged.

D514: Dynamic mirror and spline curvature combs (PHASE2_SPEC P2.8 items 4 and 10). (1) Dynamic Mirror (`sketch.dynamic_mirror`) is a checkable command in the Sketch tab Mirror dropdown. Turning it on with one sketch line or centerline selected uses that line as the mirror line; otherwise the status bar asks "Dynamic mirror: click a line or centerline to mirror about. Escape cancels." and the next click on a line picks it (Escape cancels and turns it off). With no sketch open the command stays off and the status bar says to open a sketch first. (2) While it is on, each shape a drawing tool makes is mirrored about the line at once with the core mirror operation (`core/sketch/ops/mirror.py`): the copies get Symmetric relations to the originals, in the same undo step as the shape. Points, lines, circles and arcs are mirrored, each end point with its own Symmetric relation, so the copy is fully set by the original and adds no degrees of freedom; splines and ellipses are not (the core mirror does not handle them) and the status bar says "Dynamic mirror skips splines and ellipses. Use Mirror for other shapes." Fixed reference copies of part geometry are not mirrored. A pick tool (Trim, Offset, patterns and so on) is not mirrored: only shapes made by the drawing tools' clicks are. The mirror line is kept on the open sketch (`SketchEdit.mirror_axis`). (3) It stays on until the command is used again, the mirror line is deleted, or the sketch closes. The sketch status bar adds "Dynamic mirror on." while it is on. Nothing is saved in files. (4) Show curvature: a "Show curvature" tick box in the sketch panel, shown while the Spline tool is active or a spline is selected, and the matching View > Hide or Show item "Curvature Combs" (`view.curvature_combs`). When on, every spline in the sketch canvas draws its curvature comb: at 60 samples per spline the curve normal scaled by the curvature, with the tips joined. The scale makes the longest tooth a fixed share (one fifth) of the spline's size. The samples, tangents and curvature are computed with numpy from the Bezier pieces (`core/sketch/freeform.curvature_comb`). (5) The combs are view state only (not saved), kept for the session like the relation tags.
Status: Default
Reason: Mirroring each new shape with the existing mirror operation gives the same relations as the Mirror tool; combs as view state need no file change.

## b. CHANGELOG (Added)

- Sketch: 3 Point Corner Rectangle, 3 Point Center Rectangle, Parallelogram and Perimeter Circle tools; an option to open the size box at the cursor while drawing.
- Sketch: Dynamic Mirror mirrors each new shape about a line as you draw, and splines can show curvature combs.

## c. User guide

Page: docs/user_guide/sketching.md (drawing tools and preferences sections).

- 3 Point Corner Rectangle: click two corners to set one side at any angle, then click to set the other side.
- 3 Point Center Rectangle: click the centre, click the middle of one side, then click to set the other size.
- Parallelogram: click two corners, then click the third corner anywhere.
- Hold Shift on the last click of a 3 point rectangle to make a square.
- Perimeter Circle: click three points on the circle. Points in a line give no circle.
- Preferences > Sketch > On screen value input while drawing: the size box opens at the cursor as soon as a shape starts. Type a size and press Enter, or keep drawing with the mouse.
- Dynamic Mirror: select a line or centerline and click Dynamic Mirror (or click it and then the line). Every shape you draw is mirrored about the line until you click Dynamic Mirror again.
- Show curvature: tick it in the sketch panel with a spline selected, or use View > Hide or Show > Curvature Combs. Longer teeth mean a tighter bend.

## d. Left out

- Splines and ellipses drawn while Dynamic Mirror is on are not mirrored: the core mirror operation has no spline or ellipse copy. The status bar says so.
- The mirror copy of a line chain has its joins held by the Symmetric relations, not by its own Coincident relations (adding them would over define the sketch).
- Perimeter Circle has no typed size (no single size to type while its points are picked).
