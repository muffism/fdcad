# Round 9t group D2: Hole Tool slots and revolve Thin Feature

Decisions D523 (revolve Thin Feature) and D524 (Hole Tool slots). Written before
the code and updated to match what was built.

## a. Decisions

D523: Thin Feature for Revolve and Revolved Cut (TOOL_SPEC T6.1 item 5). (1) Revolve takes the extrude Thin Feature keys unchanged (D211): `thin_type` (`"one_direction"`, `"mid_plane"`, `"two_direction"`), `thin_thickness`, `thin_thickness2`, `thin_reverse`, `thin_cap` and `thin_cap_thickness`, all optional and written only when Thin Feature is on, so older files save byte for byte the same. (2) The wall is made exactly as for the extrude (`extrude_thin.thin_sketch`): the region between the curves offset to the two sides in the sketch plane, One Direction inside a closed profile and on the offset side of an open one, Reverse swaps the sides, Mid Plane splits the thickness, Two Direction takes the first thickness outside and the second inside. Open profiles (one chain without branches) are allowed only with Thin Feature on. A sketch line used as the axis is left out of the chain. A wall from a single straight line is offset in the sketch plane by the code (the kernel finds no plane for a lone line, so before this both Extrude and Revolve failed with "the open profile could not be offset"); One Direction puts it to the right of the line's direction seen from the sketch normal, Reverse to the left. (3) The wall is then turned with the revolve's own angles and end conditions (Blind, Mid Plane, Direction 2, Through All, Up to Vertex, Up to Face, Offset from Face), boss or cut, Merge Result and Cut Outside as before. Selected Regions are not used while Thin Feature is on, as for the extrude. (4) The wall must stay on one side of the axis (it may touch it): otherwise "Revolve 1 failed: the thin wall crosses the axis. Make the wall thinner, put it on the other side with Reverse, or move the profile away from the axis." (5) Face names follow D211: `side/<entity>/outer`, `side/<entity>/inner`, the end walls of an open profile `side/<entity>/start` and `side/<entity>/end`, and the angular ends `cap/start` and `cap/end` as before. A full turn of a thin wall revolves a copy of the profile (`solids.revolve(copy=True)`): only then does the kernel report the faces made by edges square to the axis, which would otherwise be left as `face#k`. Plain revolves keep the old call so old files keep their face names (see d). (6) Cap ends: closed profiles only, and only for a revolve of less than a full turn whose ends are given by angles (Blind, Mid Plane, Direction 2, Up to Vertex, or a face plane that holds the axis). Each end gets a flat lid of the cap thickness: the region inside the wall's outer side (as for the extrude) made into a slab from the end face into the revolve, and clipped to that region turned through the whole angle, so it never pokes out near the axis or past the outer wall. The lids' outer faces join `cap/start` and `cap/end`, their inner faces are `thin_cap/start` and `thin_cap/end`, their other faces `lid_side/...` and `lid_clip/...`. Otherwise "Revolve 1: Cap ends needs a revolve of less than 360 degrees with angle ends. Turn it off." (7) This replaces the last sentence of D211 ("Thin Feature is not offered for revolve, since P2.1 names only the extrude"). (8) The panel shows the Thin Feature group for Revolve and Revolved Cut, the same group the extrude uses (`ThinControls`). (9) The build123d script export writes "Not exported: revolve Thin Feature." for a thin revolve.
Status: Default
Reason: Reusing the extrude wall keeps one set of rules and keys; a lid clipped to the turned region is the closest match to the extrude's Cap ends.

D524: Hole Tool slots (PHASE4_SPEC P4.1 item 2). (1) Simple, Counterbore and Countersink holes get a Slot tick box: optional key `slot` (true, written only when ticked) with `slot_span` (length), `slot_span_mode` (`"overall"`, written only then; centre to centre by default) and `slot_angle` (angle in the face plane from the position sketch's X axis, written only when not zero). The length key is not `slot_length` because the nut slot already uses that name for a different length. A boolean on the three hole types fits the existing layout better than three new types: the standard tables, sizes, fits and type groups stay as they are. (2) Centre to centre: the distance between the two end centres, greater than zero. Overall: end to end over the round ends, greater than the hole diameter; the centre distance is the overall length less the diameter. Errors: "Hole 1: the slot length must be greater than zero. Enter a longer slot." and "Hole 1: the overall slot length must be greater than the hole diameter. Enter a longer slot or measure it centre to centre." (3) Each position point is the slot centre. The slot runs along the position sketch's X axis turned by `slot_angle`, laid flat on the plane square to the hole axis. (4) Shape: the hole's own section (head, counterbore, countersink, wall, flat or drill point bottom, head clearance, far side countersink) is swept straight along the centre line between the two end centres, and each end is the same section turned half a turn, so every level of the hole is a stadium (two half circles joined by straight sides). The pieces are fused and merged into one tool per position, and all positions are still cut in one boolean (D313 item 5). (5) Depths (Through All, Up to Next, Up to Vertex, Up to Face) are found along the axis at the slot centre, as for a round hole at that point. (6) Fit presets and the printer tolerance widen the slot like a round hole: the diameters grow by the fit; the centre distance stays, so an overall length also grows by the fit. (7) Face names: the straight sides are `hole/<p>/<role>/side1` and `side2`, the round ends `hole/<p>/<role>/end1` and `end2` (roles as for round holes: `wall`, `cbore_wall`, `csk`, `far_csk`, `tip`, `head_wall`); flat faces across the slot keep the round hole's names (`hole/<p>/bottom`, `cbore_floor`, `head`). (8) Slots are not offered for tapped holes, heat set inserts, nut traps or nut slots; the core ignores `slot` on those types. Cosmetic and modelled threads do not apply.
Status: Default
Reason: Slots let a printed mount slide to adjust; building them from the round hole's own section keeps every hole option working on slots.

## b. CHANGELOG (Added)

- Hole Tool slots: Simple, Counterbore and Countersink holes can be slots, with a length measured centre to centre or overall and an angle in the face plane (D524).
- Thin Feature for Revolve and Revolved Cut, including open profiles and Cap ends (D523).

## c. User guide

Page `docs/user_guide/features.md` (Hole Tool section):

Slots. Tick Slot on the Type tab of a Simple, Counterbore or Countersink hole to make a slot at each position. Enter the slot length and choose how it is measured: Centre to centre (between the centres of the two round ends) or Overall (end to end). Slot angle turns the slot in the face plane; 0 runs it along the X axis of the position sketch. The printing fit widens the slot like a round hole.

Page `docs/user_guide/features.md` (Revolve section):

Thin Feature. Tick Thin Feature to revolve a wall around the sketch curves instead of the regions they enclose, as for Extrude. An open profile, such as an L shaped line, makes a cup or a dish. The wall must not cross the axis. Cap ends closes both ends of a revolve of less than 360 degrees with a lid.

## d. Left out and notes

1. Slot depths for Through All, Up to Next, Up to Vertex and Up to Face are found along the axis at the slot centre only. A slot that crosses a step in the part is cut to the depth found at its centre; Through All is long enough to go through anyway.
2. Slots are not offered for tapped holes (as the brief says), heat set inserts, nut traps and nut slots.
3. The Hole Tool has no drag handle for the slot length or angle; the values are typed in the panel.
4. Revolve Cap ends is refused for a full turn (there are no angular ends) and for ends on a plane that does not hold the axis (no single end angle).
5. Existing bug found, not fixed here (outside this group, and fixing it renames faces in old files): a plain full turn Revolve leaves the faces made by profile edges square to the axis unnamed (`<fid>/face#k`), for example the flat top and bottom of a revolved ring. `solids.revolve(..., copy=True)` names them; the bug hunt can decide whether to switch plain revolves over with a file version note.
6. `extrude_thin._offset_open` now takes the sketch plane (needed for the single line fix); the only callers are in `extrude_thin`.
