# UI review of 2026-11-19

Owner request: the UI "looks like a million bucks; make it look like a billion bucks". This page is a critical review of the look as it stood at commit `9056d798`, and a record of what was changed on the `ui-polish` branch. It is FDCAD's own review of FDCAD's own screens. Nothing in it comes from another program (CLAUDE.md sections 7 and 10).

## How it was done

1. `tools/ui_review_shots.py` opens the main window on Qt's offscreen platform and saves about 40 pictures per theme: the start page (with and without recent parts), the main window with the enclosure sample, every command tab, the tree, the status bar, the Extrude, Fillet and Hole Tool panels, sketch mode, the right click menus of the tree, the view and the sketch, the first three menus of the menu bar, message bars, the Errors and Variables tabs, every page of Preferences, a message window, the node editor and a sheet of every control in its normal, disabled, checked and keyboard focus look.
2. It was run in the four themes at 100 percent, and in Light and Dark at 150 percent.
3. The 3D view is OpenGL and draws blank offscreen, so the review is of everything round it. The view's own colours, lighting and edges were not judged.
4. Sizes were measured from the widgets, not guessed from the pictures.

Run it again with `uv run python tools/ui_review_shots.py --out .shots/after`. The `.shots` folder is ignored by git. Before and after pairs are in `docs/screenshots/ui_review_2026-11-19/`.

## Verdict

The Datum look holds. The palette, the two radii, the type scale, the icon set and the command tabs are consistent in all four themes, and the rules of `UI_POLISH_SPEC.md` are mostly kept. What cost polish was not the system but the places it had not reached: the newest panel (the node editor), the corners Qt lays out itself (tab corner widgets, group boxes, spin boxes, message windows), and screens that are seen rarely (empty states, the Errors tab). Twenty three faults follow, all fixed on this branch. Ten more points are proposals, listed with the reason each was left.

## Findings, most costly first

Each finding says where it is, what shows, why it costs polish, and the exact change. "Done" names the decision that records it.

### A. Things that were wrong

**A1. Node titles could not be read.** Node editor, every node. The coloured band behind a node's title was painted only in its top half; the white title stood half on the pale card. Why: the title is the one thing a node must say. The band was two overlapping shapes filled with the odd even rule, which leaves the overlap empty. Change: fill the band with the winding rule. Done, D2690, with a test that reads the band's pixels.

**A2. The tick on OK was invisible.** Property panel header. OK had an accent coloured tick on an accent filled button, so nothing showed and the word OK sat off centre; Cancel beside it had a visible red cross. Why: an icon that cannot be seen is noise, and the main button looked misprinted. Change: OK and Cancel are words only, as in every window. Done, D2692.

**A3. The Errors tab was a different grey from the tree.** Left panel. The Errors page, and the margins of other pages, had the window colour (`#E9ECF1` in Light) while the tree had the panel colour (`#F5F6F9`). Why: DESIGN_LANGUAGE 3.2 says the left panel is one colour from its tab row to the bottom; switching tabs flashed the panel darker. The style rule named a widget that does not exist. Change: colour the stack that holds every page. Done, D2693.

**A4. The start page was the wrong colour.** Start page. Its style rule asks for the panel colour, but the rule was never painted, so the page was the window's darker grey. Why: the middle of the window flipped from dark to light when a part opened. Change: let the widget paint its style sheet background. Done, D2695.

**A5. The search box hung low and ran off the edge.** Top right of the main window. "Search tools" sat 6 px lower than the tab names, 1 px above the card of tools, with no margin at the window's edge. The small arrow that lists the tabs, at the top left, hung low in the same way. Why: three things in one row on three centre lines. Change: a 6 px margin below and at the right of the box, the margins a tab has; the same below the arrow. Done, D2691.

**A6. Message windows used stock pictures.** Any message window (unsaved changes, errors). Qt's glossy yellow triangle, the same in all four themes, beside FDCAD's line icons. Why: the one picture in the window is not FDCAD's. Change: the style gives FDCAD's warning, error, information and question marks in theme colours. Done, D2696. Two new icons, `ui.info_mark` and `ui.question_mark`, drawn for this.

**A7. The second card of the Gestures page was cut off.** Preferences, Gestures. The Part and Sketch cards together were wider than the window, and the page does not scroll sideways, so the Sketch card ran off the right edge and the arrows of its drop downs could not be reached. Why: a control that cannot be reached. Each drop down took the width of its longest command. Change: the drop downs stretch with their card; the list that drops down keeps its full width. Done, D2699.

### B. Spacing and alignment on the grid

**B1. Fields in a group did not share a left edge.** Extrude, Fillet and every panel built from the same helpers. A row that starts with a field icon put its field 24 px in; a row that starts with the Reverse Direction button put it 33 px in. The field icons also sat 4 px above the middle of their fields. Why: U2.3 asks for one left edge; the ragged edge is the first thing seen in a column of fields. Change: a field icon gets one cell the size of a small tool button, with the icon in its middle. Done, D2692. Left: rows with a word in front (Along; Type, Standard and Size in the Hole Tool) keep their own label column, so a group that mixes the two kinds still has two edges. See P6.

**B2. The sketch panel did not follow the property panel.** Sketch mode, left panel. OK and Cancel were right aligned at their smallest size where the property panel spreads them across the panel; the body had 8 px padding where the header has 12; its groups had the title outside the card in the normal weight. Why: two panels that open in the same place taught two layouts. Change: the same two button row, 12 px padding, and group boxes drawn as cards with a semibold title inside. Done, D2692. The group box change applies to every window that uses one.

**B3. "Appearances" started 4 px left of its neighbours.** Feature tree. Its icon was a bare 14 px square among 18 px icons. Why: one row out of line in a list read as a fault. Change: the swatch is drawn in a cell of the row icon size, rounded, with a thin edge. Done, D2693.

**B4. Spin boxes were taller than the fields beside them, and their numbers hugged the edge.** Preferences (Arrow key turn, Mouse turn speed) and any form with a spin box. 28 px against 25 px, and the number 3 px from the edge against 9 px. Why: rows of a form stepped up and down, and numbers did not line up in a column. Change: 1 px of vertical padding and a 6 px text margin. Done, D2697. A spin box is now 26 px, 1 px more than a text field; closing that last pixel needs the inner field resized in code and is not worth it.

**B5. Gaps off the grid.** The tree's filter box and state choice were 2 px apart. Message bars had padding of 8, 4, 4, 4 with 2 px between bars and fixed 6 px corners. Why: U2.2 puts every gap on 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, and fixed pixels do not grow with a larger font. Change: all of these come from the spacing step. Done, D2694 and D2697.

### C. Hierarchy and states

**C1. Message bars had three buttons of one weight and no mark.** Top of the view. Suppress, Don't Show Again and Close were three outlined buttons; a warning and a notice differed only by the colour of a 4 px edge. Why: the action the bar exists for did not lead, and U4.4 forbids colour alone. Change: a mark at the left; the action stays outlined, the other two are flat. The bars became cards with 8 px of air at the sides. Done, D2694.

**C2. Lists that take picks had no outline.** Fillet (Items to Fillet), Preferences (Hidden messages), the sketch panel. A grey area with no edge inside a white card. Why: U1.2 asks for a 3:1 outline on inputs; the list did not read as a place to put things. Change: the field colour and the field outline for lists inside a group, a group box or a window. Done, D2692.

**C3. Help and Pin did not match.** Property panel header. Help was a bare "?" and Pin a 90 px wide button. Why: the smaller control was the larger target, and the pair pushed the feature's name into a corner. Change: two small outlined buttons of one height. Done, D2692. Their words are unchanged. See P5.

**C4. The progress bar's percent could not be read.** Status bar. Dark text over the accent fill, in a bar shorter than its letters. Why: under 3:1, and clipped. Change: a slim bar with no figure; the status text names the step. Done, D2697.

**C5. The node list was one run of text.** Node editor. "Boolean Toggle    Input" with the category wherever the title ended, and a sideways scroll bar. Why: the eye could not scan either column. Change: title at the left, category at the right in the muted colour, no sideways scroll. Done, D2690.

**C6. The node editor's title bar was unstyled.** Node editor. Fusion's small grey float and close pictures and a normal weight title. Why: the only title bar in the app, and the only one not in the look. Change: semibold title and FDCAD's own two marks. Done, D2690. Qt draws these two buttons 10 px wide whatever the style says. See P7.

**C7. Ticks in lists were not FDCAD's check boxes.** Preferences, Shortcut Bar, and any list, tree or table with tick boxes. Rows showed Fusion's small black box beside check boxes with the accent fill. Why: one kind of control with two looks (U3.1). Change: the same size, outline, fill and tick for tick boxes in rows. Done, D2699.

### D. Empty states

**D1. The start page with no recent part was a blank card.** Start page, first run. A white card of about 1100 by 500 px with one line of text under it. Why: U8.3; the first screen a new user sees said nothing where they were looking. Change: the words are centred where the parts will be, in a dashed outline. Done, D2695.

**D2. "No warnings or errors." was a stray line.** Errors tab. Plain text at the left edge, part way down. Change: centred, muted, under the two ticks. Done, D2693.

**D3. The Errors table hid its message.** Errors tab at the default panel width. The Message column showed two letters. Change: the Kind column is as wide as its word. Done, D2693. At 264 px the message is still short; the tooltip and the detail text under the table carry it.

### E. High DPI

**E1. Five pictures were soft at 150 and 200 percent.** The mark on the start page and in About, relation icons in the sketch panel and the Relations panel, marks in the tree's problem box. They asked for a 100 percent picture, which Qt stretched. Change: they ask for the screen's pixel ratio; a test scans the UI code for the plain form. Done, D2698.

**E2. Everything else scaled cleanly.** At 150 percent no text clipped, no widgets overlapped and every gap grew with the scale in the screens above. The Sketch tab is wider than a 1440 px window and scrolls, which D1034 already records; at 1680 px it fits.

## Proposed, needs the owner

These were not done on the `ui-polish` branch. Each changes a colour, a label, a tested behaviour or a spec that is the owner's to decide. The owner approved P1, P4, P5 and P9 the same day; they were done on the `ui-polish-2` branch and are marked below. See "Second pass" at the end. The owner then approved the other six, P2, P3, P6, P7, P8 and P10; they were done on the `ui-polish-3` branch. See "Third pass". One point found in that pass, P11, was new; the owner approved it on 2026-11-20 and it was done on the `ui-leftovers` branch. See "Fourth pass". No proposal is open.

**P1. The information bar's green edge.** An information bar takes its edge and tint from `state_ok`, the green of "this worked" (D1034 item 12). A notice such as "This part was opened to look at" is not a success. Proposal: a neutral edge in the muted text colour, or cobalt. This is a change of colour use, so it waits for the owner (D745). Done in the second pass, D2700: a neutral edge.

**P2. Accent text on hover and selected rows.** Still 4.20 to 4.48 against the 4.5 limit in Beige, Light and Dark (D745, `contrast.open_pairs`). Not touched. Proposal, as before: darken the Light and Beige accent used for text by one step, or lighten those two fills. Done in the third pass, D2740, with no colour changed: measured again, the pairs already pass (4.61 to 6.91). The figures above were those of D745, from before the Datum colours.

**P3. The unit selector's outline.** The drop down at the right of the status bar has a 1.3:1 outline; U1.2 asks 3:1 for a drop down. Raising it makes the selector the loudest thing in the status bar. Proposal: keep it quiet and note the exception in U1.2, or raise it to `input_border`. Either is the owner's call because the spec and the look disagree. Done in the third pass, D2741: raised to `input_border`.

**P4. Icons in the right click menus.** The menu bar's menus show an icon beside most commands; the tree's and the view's right click menus show none for the same commands (Edit Feature, Suppress, Delete, Zoom to Fit). Proposal: give each right click item the icon of its command. Left because it touches every item of `context_menu.py` and the tree menus, and several commands have no icon yet. Done in the second pass, D2703: entries without an icon stay without one.

**P5. Three labels that could be icons.** "Close" on a message bar could be a cross with the same tooltip; "Pin" in the panel header could be a pin; "?" could be the help icon. Each saves width in a narrow panel. Label changes need the owner, and a pin icon would be a new drawing. Done in the second pass, D2701.

**P6. One label column for a whole panel.** Rows with a word in front (Along, Type, Standard, Size) and rows with an icon in front still start their fields at two different places inside one group. Proposal: every row gets an icon cell and the word moves into the field's caption or tooltip, or every row gets a word. This rebuilds the forms and changes labels. Done in the third pass, D2742, without rebuilding a form or changing a label: one column for the whole panel.

**P7. A title bar of FDCAD's own for the node editor.** Qt fixes the float and close buttons at 10 px, under the 24 px target of U3.3. A custom title bar with two 24 px buttons fixes that, but replaces Qt's own dragging and double click of the panel, which is behaviour. Done in the third pass, D2745: Qt's dragging and double click are kept.

**P8. The same header for the Reference Geometry and Section View panels.** They still have OK and Cancel with icons in one row. Proposal: move them to the two row header of D2692. Left out to keep this branch to the panels a user sees most. Done in the third pass, D2743.

**P9. Three more empty states.** With no part open the left panel shows a filter box over nothing; the Variables table is a blank white area; the sketch panel shows an empty "Add Relation" card and an empty "Relations of the selection" list before anything is picked. Proposal: one muted line each, and hide the two sketch cards until there is a selection. The first two need new wording; the third is pinned by `test_tool_panel_shows_while_a_tool_is_active`. Done in the second pass, D2702: the two sketch cards stay in view and say what they will show.

**P10. The arrow under a one line label in the command tabs.** On Fillet and Plane the dropdown arrow sits a full line under the label, because all arrows share one line and other labels have two lines. RIBBON_SPEC R3.1 says the arrow sits under the label. Both readings are defensible; the command tabs belong to RIBBON_SPEC (U3.6), so it is left. Done in the third pass, D2744: the arrow sits under the last line of its label, recorded in RIBBON_SPEC as R3B.

**P11. Coloured text on the pressed fill.** New, found in the third pass while measuring P2. A selected row shows the pressed fill while the pointer is on it, and U1.1 names pressed fills. Plain text on it passes. Coloured text does not everywhere: in Beige accent 3.98 and the "ok" green 4.38; in Light accent 4.16 and danger 4.49; in Dark muted 3.95, accent 3.70, danger 4.00 and error 4.06, all against 4.5. Stealth passes. No row shows accent text today; muted, danger and state text do show on rows. Proposal: a pressed fill a little lighter in Beige and Light (`#C3D2F0` and `#C5D6FF` today) and darker in Dark (`#2C4886` today), or a fill of its own for a selected row under the pointer. Either makes pressed look closer to selected, which is a change of look, so it waits for the owner (D745). `contrast.open_pressed_pairs` lists the pairs with a strict expected failure. Done in the fourth pass, D2840: new pressed fills in Light, Beige and Dark; every pair is 4.52 or more and the test passes without its mark.

## Second pass: P1, P4, P5 and P9

The owner approved four of the proposals on 2026-11-19. They were done on the `ui-polish-2` branch, on top of the first pass. `tests/ui/test_ui_polish_2.py` holds one check or more per change.

1. **P1, information bars (D2700).** The left edge of an information bar is the muted text colour, not the green of `state_ok`. A green edge now means success only. Neutral was chosen over cobalt: cobalt is kept for what a tool acts on, and with the colour vision safe preference the "ok" colour is itself a blue. The contrast helper measures the new edge.
2. **P4, right click menus (D2703).** An entry shows the icon its command has in the menu bar, the command tabs and the context toolbar. No icon was drawn. An entry whose command has none stays without one, and its text stays in line, because every menu row keeps the icon column.

Each keeps its word as the accessible name and at the start of its tooltip, and a 24 px target. Two icons were drawn for FDCAD: `ui.pin` and `ui.pin_on`.
4. **P9, empty states (D2702).** The left panel with no part open, the Variables table with no variables, and the two relation cards of the sketch panel each say what will show in them. The Add Relation card stays in view, as its test pins.

## Fourth pass: P11 and three leftovers of the narrow panels

The owner approved P11, colour change included, on 2026-11-20, with the three points the look review of the narrow panels had left alone (D2808 points 2 to 4). They were done on the `ui-leftovers` branch. `tests/ui/test_ui_leftovers.py` holds one check or more per change; P11 is checked in `tests/ui/test_theme_contrast.py`, and the sweep of `tests/ui/test_panel_widths.py` looks at every tool's panel for the second and third point.

1. **P11, the pressed fill (D2840).** A selected row under the pointer has a new fill in three themes: Light `#C5D6FF` to `#D1DFFF`, Beige `#C3D2F0` to `#D1DFFD`, Dark `#2C4886` to `#1B3980`. Stealth passed and keeps `#1C376B`. The eight pairs that were 3.70 to 4.49 are 4.52 to 4.97, and no text colour changed. The accent sets the limit: a fill that keeps accent text at 4.5:1 can be only about 2 L* from the selected fill, where it was 5 to 7. So each new fill takes that step and is a stronger blue as well, on the hue it had. A selected row under the pointer is quieter than before and still a different row; a test holds that too, so the two fills cannot become one. A pressed button has the same fill.
2. **Labels, not field names (D2841).** Split's list of bodies read "keep". It reads Keep, which sits in the column of Trim tools, Bodies and Tolerance; "Bodies to keep" was tried and pushed the list 29 px out of that column. Six more bare names were found and are words now: Keep (Intersect), Angle (Circular Pattern), Type and Pin (Hinge), An axis (Move Face, Offset Face) and Flat (Gusset).
3. **Empty rows (D2842).** A note with no words (the Hole Tool's fit note, Draft's note under Add Faces That Need Draft, Shell's answer under Check Walls) took a line and the gap of a row. So did a holder of rows with no row in it (Shell's Multi Thickness, Fillet's Variable Radii). Each takes its row when it has something to show.
4. **Fillet's Variable Radii (D2843).** The fields of "Point 1 at (%)" and of "Edge 1 point 1" start at one x; they were 8 px apart.

One fault was found on the way and fixed: Variable Radii had rows for only as many edges as it had control points (D2844). The "after" pictures of the panel groups show the radii of all four edges for that reason.

No command or shortcut changed. Three theme colours changed, all `pressed`. No dependency was added. Nothing in the 3D view or its theme tokens was touched.

Seen and left: in the offscreen font, which is much wider than a real one, a label that breaks in two lines can be laid out one line high until the next change (Curve Array, "Instances to skip"). No real font breaks that label (D2842).
